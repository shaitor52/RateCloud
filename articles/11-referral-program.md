# Referral program: invite people to try Rate Cloud

English · [Русский](../ru/articles/11-referral-program.md)

Rate Cloud's referral program lets you recommend the service and receive bonuses from eligible subscription payments. Participation is free and voluntary; buying a subscription is not required to participate. There is no payment just for a registration, an invitation, or joining the program.

These terms summarize the [official service agreement](https://rate-cloud.com/offer), checked November 3, 2026. The agreement and dashboard govern eligibility, calculation, and withdrawal.

## Benefits for an invited new user

A new account registered through an eligible personal invitation receives:

- **A five-hour trial instead of the standard three hours.** Email verification, Telegram linking, trial eligibility checks, and applicable IP restrictions still apply.
- **10% off the first subscription purchase.** The discount applies only to the first purchase and does not combine with promotional codes.

The extended trial and discount require a valid referral registration. An ordinary visit to the documentation or the general registration link does not itself apply somebody's invitation. Existing accounts cannot be attached to a referrer retrospectively.

## Bonuses for recommendations

| Relationship | Published bonus from eligible payments |
| --- | ---: |
| An account you directly invited | 10% |
| Second step of the recommendation chain | 3% |
| Third step of the recommendation chain | 1% |

The chain stops after three steps. Bonuses apply to eligible payments, including repeat purchases, rather than only the first purchase. These are percentages of actual qualifying payments, not guaranteed income or a fixed return.

Only money actually received through the payment service qualifies. Portions paid with bonuses, gift codes, compensation, and operator grants do not generate bonuses. A bonus becomes available after fourteen days; before then it is pending. Refunded or reversed payments reverse the related accruals, and a negative balance can suspend withdrawals until it is covered.

## How attribution works

Your personal link is available in the **Referrals** section of the account dashboard. A visit through a link can be retained in the browser for up to 180 days. If several links were visited before registration, the first one takes precedence. The referral relationship is assigned once when the new account is registered and does not change afterward.

## Use and withdrawal

The bonus balance accumulates without expiry. It can cover up to 100% of an order at checkout. The dashboard records accruals and deductions. Displayed amounts and accounting rules follow the current terms; a displayed USD equivalent is informational.

Withdrawals are requested through support to a bank card or in cryptocurrency once the balance meets the published minimum equivalent to **USD 30**. Conversion, identity checks, payment eligibility, and tax responsibilities follow the current terms. Do not assume an automatic or instant payout.

Bonuses are credited to users with verified email and linked Telegram. Self-referrals, multiple-account abuse, artificial purchases and returns, spam, misleading claims, and other manipulation are prohibited. Invited users' identities are partly masked in the dashboard.

## Referral program and White Label serve different needs

Referrals are personal recommendations using the program's own attribution and bonus rules. [White Label](07-white-label.md) is a separate offering for selling subscriptions under your own brand, with an agreed setup and partner markup. Do not combine the two sets of benefits or promise that their commercial rules stack.

## Start here

[Create your Rate Cloud account](https://rate-cloud.com/register) or [sign in](https://rate-cloud.com/login), then open the Referrals section to find your own link. If somebody invited you, use their actual personal link before creating the account.

For the ordinary trial, [register and complete verification](https://rate-cloud.com/register). For a branded business, [visit the official White Label page](https://rate-cloud.com/en/white-label). [Read the full referral terms](https://rate-cloud.com/offer).
