White Label: an AI subscription service under your brand

Rate Cloud's White Label offering lets a partner sell model subscriptions under their own brand while Rate Cloud supplies and maintains the technical service. The partner focuses on branding, pricing, and customer acquisition.

What the published offer includes

The website describes a branded website and customer dashboard, a domain and hosting, payments, a partner panel, a Telegram bot, customer technical support, model infrastructure, updates, and fixes. Branding changes include the logo, colors, fonts, and other agreed design elements. The final scope is agreed before launch.

The public page also presents application and assistant concepts. It explicitly identifies the illustrated interfaces as demonstrations based on OpenCode and describes shared applications with neutral branding. Those illustrations should not be interpreted as an independently verified release of a separate native app for every partner.

Commercial model

As published on November 3, 2026, setup costs $98.80 once, with no mandatory monthly maintenance fee. The USD setup amount is a dated display equivalent using the website's currency metadata on November 3, 2026; confirm the current price before purchase. Model subscriptions still have their base cost. Included maintenance does not make access to the models free.

A partner adds a markup to the base Rate Cloud subscription price. The website describes partner accruals on each paid subscription, including repeat purchases, and monthly payouts. Advertising, taxes, and other partner expenses remain separate.

For the site's example, a $34.38 base subscription with a 20% markup produces a $41.26 customer price and a $6.88 partner accrual before the partner's own expenses and taxes. A 20% markup on cost is not a 20% margin on the final selling price. Sales volume and profit are not guaranteed.

Responsibilities and launch

Rate Cloud describes responsibility for the service infrastructure, website and dashboard, domain and servers, payments, bot, model updates, fixes, and customer technical support. The partner chooses the brand, sets the markup, and develops sales and repeat purchases.

The published launch process is to agree on the brand and domain, pay the setup fee, prepare the service, and begin sales. A launch date is agreed after discussing the requirements rather than promised as a fixed universal turnaround.

For current scope, commercial terms, and a launch discussion, visit Rate Cloud White Label or contact the support account linked there.
